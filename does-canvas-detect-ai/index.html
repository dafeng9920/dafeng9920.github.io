Canvas AI detection

Does Canvas Detect AI?

In the standard LMS workflow, Canvas itself does not currently provide a built-in AI-writing detector. AI-writing detection that appears in Canvas typically comes from institution-enabled integrations such as Turnitin or Copyleaks—not from Canvas alone.

Quick summary

  • A school can enable a third-party service, while another school may not.
  • Detection can be enabled for one assignment and not another.
  • AI indicators are signals for review, not proof of misconduct.

How it actually works

Canvas can host an assignment and pass a submission to an enabled integration. The integration returns its own report to the instructor workflow. That architecture is why “Does Canvas detect AI?” cannot be answered accurately with a universal yes or no.

Canvas native features vs. third-party detection

Canvas may record submission timestamps, course activity, and quiz-related metadata. Those records can support an instructor’s review, but they are not AI-writing detection and do not prove AI use. Text-based similarity or AI indicators come from a separate, enabled service.

What instructors may see

Depending on the institution, license, assignment settings, and integration, an instructor may see a similarity report, an AI-writing indicator, a third-party report, or Canvas activity metadata. An instructor view is not automatically the student view; student visibility depends on the integration and assignment settings.

What Canvas cannot prove

Canvas activity data cannot establish that a person used ChatGPT. Nor does an AI-detector signal identify a specific model. Detection outputs are probabilistic indicators and should prompt careful human review rather than function as an automatic verdict.

2026 update: Document Processor workflows

Canvas’s newer Document Processor workflow can let instructors use Turnitin from standard Canvas assignment settings and view results in SpeedGrader. This is an additional workflow, not a replacement for every older Turnitin setup. It should also be kept distinct from Turnitin LTI 1.3 and the legacy Plagiarism Framework, which are separate integration paths.

FAQ

Does Canvas automatically check every assignment for AI?

No. Whether a third-party service is used depends on the institution and the relevant assignment’s settings.

Can an AI score prove academic misconduct?

No. It is a signal that may require context and human review.
