Canvas architecture

What AI Detector Does Canvas Use?

Canvas does not use one universal AI detector. What appears in a Canvas course depends on the institution’s configuration and enabled third-party integrations. Turnitin and Copyleaks are two documented integration examples.

Quick summary

  • Canvas is the LMS; detection services are separate products.
  • Turnitin and Copyleaks can be institution-enabled integrations.
  • Workflow, license, and assignment settings affect what an instructor sees.

A Canvas institution decides which integrations it licenses and enables. A teacher may then have assignment-level options. The presence of Canvas in a course does not by itself show that any detector is active.

Turnitin

Turnitin provides Canvas integration paths, including LTI 1.3. Its reports can be available to instructors in the Canvas grading workflow. Its legacy Plagiarism Framework path should not be treated as the same workflow as LTI 1.3.

Copyleaks

Copyleaks documents a Canvas LTI 1.3 integration for its plagiarism and AI-detection services. Whether it is active in a particular course still depends on institution and course configuration.

2026 update: the Document Processor

Canvas’s Document Processor is a newer route that can bring Turnitin processing into a standard Canvas assignment workflow, with results available through SpeedGrader. It is separate from both Turnitin LTI 1.3 and the legacy Plagiarism Framework—not a label for one single unified workflow. Adoption is institution-specific.

What reports mean—and do not mean

A third-party report can support an instructor’s review. It does not prove that a student used a particular AI model or establish misconduct on its own. Activity metadata may add context, but it is not AI-writing detection.

FAQ

Does every Canvas school use Turnitin?

No. Turnitin requires an institution’s license and configuration; Canvas does not make it universally active.

Are Turnitin LTI, the legacy framework, and Document Processor the same?

No. They are distinct integration or workflow paths and should not be collapsed into one process.
