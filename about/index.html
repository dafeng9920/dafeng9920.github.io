Clear explanations of LMS AI-detection behavior

This site provides clear, source-backed explanations of LMS and AI-detection behavior.

Our approach

We distinguish platform features from institution-enabled integrations, describe uncertainty where it matters, and link to publicly available vendor and institutional documentation. We do not provide tools or instructions for bypassing academic-integrity systems.

Scope

Exact behavior may vary by institution, license, assignment settings, and enabled integrations. AI-detection indicators are not automatic findings of misconduct.
